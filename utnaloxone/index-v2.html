How it works

Three steps to save a life.

You don't need to be a doctor. If you can follow three steps, you can reverse an overdose.

01
Step 1 of 3
01

Recognize the signs

Slow or stopped breathing, blue or gray lips, pinpoint pupils, no response to their name or a shake. When in doubt, act.

02

Give the naloxone

Spray one dose into a nostril, or inject it. It's designed so anyone can use it correctly the very first time.

03

Call 911 & stay

Call for help, give a second dose after 2–3 minutes if needed, and stay until help arrives. Utah's Good Samaritan law protects you.
