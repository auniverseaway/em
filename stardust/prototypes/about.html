About · Colophon

I'm Chris.

A working publication on content management — Adobe Experience Manager and Edge Delivery in particular. Independent of Adobe, where I work. Loyal to humans making things.

01

Why this exists.

I started Experience Managed because the public writing about CMS work — especially AEM and Edge Delivery — reads like marketing collateral that someone forgot to redact. The honest version is messier, more interesting, and more useful.

I work at Adobe, but this blog is mine. I write when there's something I want in writing, and not when there isn't. The cadence is low. The signal — I hope — is high.

02

The Adobe boundary.

I'm an Adobe employee. This is not an Adobe blog.

I'll be critical of the platform when it earns it. I won't be hostile, and I won't disclose what I shouldn't. If a piece touches a project I worked on, I'll say so. If a tool ships from another team that I have feelings about, I'll write about it as a practitioner, not a coworker.

The line is "things that won't get me fired." I think that's a fine line for a writer to know about themselves.

03

On AI.

I'm not in the "real designers don't use AI" camp. I'm not in the "AI will replace us" camp either.

The right question is: are humans still trying to make things? If yes, the tool — Claude, Codex, whatever comes next — is downstream. Some of the best work I've seen this year was made by people who let the tools help. Some of the worst was made by people who let the tools decide.

The publication tries to write about both, honestly.
